Printer setup & tips

Everything for the HP Smart Tank 585.

Print from your phone over Wi-Fi

  1. Connect the phone and printer to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Install HP Smart from the Play Store and add the printer. Or enable Settings → Connected devices → Printing → Default Print Service. iPhones use AirPrint automatically.
  3. Create your PDF here and tap Share / Print, then choose HP Smart or Print.
  4. Select HP Smart Tank 580-590 series, set paper size and color, and print.

Print from a laptop

  1. Add the printer once: Windows Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers → Add device.
  2. Use Print now in this app, or open the PDF and press Ctrl + P.

Exact sizes

  1. Always set Scale 100% or Actual size. Turn off “Fit to page”.
  2. Keep Borderless off.
  3. Check your first print with a ruler: passport 3.5 × 4.5 cm, Aadhaar 8.6 × 5.4 cm.

Which paper type to choose

Your paperPaper typeQuality

For black & white prints choose Black Ink Only to save color ink.

Loading paper

  1. Load paper in portrait orientation in the rear input tray and slide the guides in.
  2. Photo paper usually goes glossy side up, facing you. Confirm once: mark an X on plain paper, load it X-up and print. If the print lands on the X, load photo paper the same way.
  3. Load no more than 10–20 sheets of photo paper at once.

Photo will not open?

Some iPhone photos are HEIC, which browsers cannot read. Set Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or add a screenshot of the photo.
